About Nat

Hi, I’m Natalia — but you can call me Nat.

I started The Hackhouse because apparently normal hobbies were not dramatic enough.

I make comedy songs, test ideas, film real-life chaos, and turn everyday moments into unnecessary productions.

Life is weird. Let’s sing about it.

I have a Master’s degree in Economics. Somehow I ended up making songs about apple trees and cankles.
